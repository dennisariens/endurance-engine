# Product specification

## Primary user

Dennis Ariens: competitive cyclist and runner balancing fixed Zwift/road events with a sub-3 marathon mission.

## Primary job

Answer, with evidence:

1. What should I do today?
2. Why?
3. What does my actual training change?
4. What must I protect over the next 72 hours?
5. Am I moving toward the active goal?

## Core capabilities

- Opening sync and trustworthy evidence status.
- Actual-activity override of planned recommendations.
- Fixed-race-aware daily coaching.
- Race-cost and recovery explanation.
- Scenario simulation for race/rest/easy/ignore choices.
- Morning readiness and next-72-hour control plan.
- Goal Readiness and Path to Goal.
- Timeline, decisions and operational evidence log.
- Private local import/export and account/data controls.

## Experience principles

- One dominant answer before deeper evidence.
- Calm, premium and precise; no cheerleading or guilt language.
- Data density only where it improves a decision.
- Expert/debug depth remains available but not dominant.

## Exclusions for baseline setup

No new coaching rule, redesign, backend migration, OAuth claim, data reset, branch promotion or deployment.
