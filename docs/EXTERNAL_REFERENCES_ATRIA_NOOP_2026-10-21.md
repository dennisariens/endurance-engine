## Transfer rules

- **Study patterns, do not copy entire applications or UX.** Atria is permissively licensed subject to notices. NOOP source is **noncommercial-licensed**: do not transplant implementation, assets or substantial code into AERION without a licensing decision/permission; independent implementation of general techniques is preferred.
- New metric-source reconciliation must be **per metric**, not a universal Garmin > WHOOP > Intervals ranking. Intervals.icu may already aggregate Garmin, Strava or Zwift; the upstream activity identity is not a second independent workout.
- Maintain precise semantics: *measured* vs *provider estimate* vs *AERION derived* vs *athlete report*; sample coverage, source and timestamp. Require 'insufficient evidence' when appropriate.
- Physiological claims must be validated against a suitable reference. App dashboards/screenshots and regression tests alone are not evidence of clinical accuracy.
- Preserve the current PR #19 authentication, revision/CAS, idempotency and provider-unavailable safeguards. **Do not assign Atria/NOOP feature work during Intervals/hosting/iPhone acceptance.**
- If AERION ever adopts a scoring approach, independently specify input schema, missing-data behavior, test fixtures, reference measurements, longitudinal evaluation, confidence and coach-facing explanation *before* permitting it to modify training.
